# Constitutional AI, mapped

A field map of Anthropic's Constitutional AI, in two parts:

- **Part A** (`paper/`): *Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback* (Bai et al., 2022),
  section by section: motivations, critique-and-revision (SL-CAI), RL from AI feedback (RLAIF),
  chain-of-thought labels, results against RLHF, and harmlessness versus evasiveness.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073
- **Part B** (`constitution/`): *Claude's Constitution* (Anthropic, January 2026): the four core
  values and their order, reasoning over rules, helpfulness and principals, honesty, harm,
  hard constraints, broad safety, and Claude's nature.
  Source: https://www.anthropic.com/constitution (CC0 1.0)
- **The bridge** (`bridge.html`): from the 2022 list of sixteen principles, through the 2023
  constitution, to the 2026 document.
- **The pipeline, explorable** (`pipeline.html`): both stages as a clickable flow with one
  running example; toggle stage 1, the principle set, chain of thought, and RLAIF vs RLHF.
  Client-side, no model calls.
- **Guides** (`guide/`): read it in 10 minutes, for builders, glossary.

**Live: https://idvorkin-ai-tools.github.io/constitutional-ai-field-map/**

Original explanation, grounded in the two sources, with at most a few short quotes per page and
section or page citations. The paper is © its authors; the constitution is CC0.
