Things to try

  • Flip to RLHF. Stage 1 disappears and the label node becomes a person. That one swap is the whole difference: the preference model and RL are unchanged (what RLAIF removes). Watch the outcome turn evasive (why).
  • Skip stage 1. No revisions means no fine-tuning data, so RL starts from a model that still writes the harmful draft, and the pair being judged gets worse.
  • Swap the constitution. Same prompt, same models, different principles: the critique, the rewrite and the label all move. The written principles are the objective.
  • Turn on chain of thought. The label becomes nearly 0 or 1; clamping pulls it back into a soft target.
